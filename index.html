Privacy Policy

App: Galeri Simülatörü (Android, com.fezainnovation.carsimulation)
Developer: Mehmet Miraç Arık
Contact: fezainnovation@gmail.com

In short

  • The game runs entirely offline and does not use an internet connection.
  • We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • There are no ads, analytics, crash reports, tracking or accounts.

What stays on your device

Your game progress (save file) and settings are stored only in the app's own folder on your device. They are never sent to any server and are excluded from Android backup. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Permissions

The app requests no Android permissions: it does not access the internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or files.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or data-collecting services. The app is distributed through Google Play; data that Google Play itself processes (such as download records) is subject to Google's privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and does not collect personal data from users of any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page and the date above will be updated.
